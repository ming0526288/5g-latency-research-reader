实际执行：

```
sudo -n /usr/bin/python3 research/latency-analysis/multi-ue/run.py --configs research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/configs-01 --output research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/n1-ul-per2-01 --ues 1 --direction UL --mode fixed_per_ue --load 2
/usr/bin/python3 research/latency-analysis/multi-ue/analyze.py research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/n1-ul-per2-01
sudo -n /usr/bin/python3 research/latency-analysis/multi-ue/run.py --configs research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/configs-01 --output research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/n2-ul-per2-01 --ues 2 --direction UL --mode fixed_per_ue --load 2
/usr/bin/python3 research/latency-analysis/multi-ue/analyze.py research/latency-analysis/results/multi-ue/n2-ul-per2-01
```

runner两次退出0；单UE4500匹配/4000统计，最差（唯一）UE P99=1.80535205ms；双UE各自完成接入/TUN与业务，gNB日志出现两个UE上下文，不是单UE多流。分析结果以measurement.json为准。两个UE流有相同seq值，分析器按不同UDP端口识别后再校验序号和发送时戳，禁止跨流误关联。

没有新建订阅/改核心网，RFsim使用独立namespace/veth；单UE及双UE运行均回收全部自有子进程，namespace删除退出0，私密身份不输出。四边界端到端MONOTONIC、分段REALTIME，局限及依据见CHG-055/056。CPU样本仅过程观测，不证明哪一层CPU瓶颈。

双UE分析器实际退出0，共9000四边界匹配/8000统计，UE0均值2.887089364ms/P99=5.70909477ms，UE1均值5.215861364ms/P99=9.18242242ms；零丢包。Jain为交付速率指标0.9999763，不证明无线PRB分配公平。安全证据通过export.py显式白名单导出到multi-ue/evidence/baseline-01，不含原配置/日志/pcap。
